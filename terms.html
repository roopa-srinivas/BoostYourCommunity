terms of use

Last updated September 23, 2026

These terms cover your use of Boost Your Community. By creating an account, you agree to them.

what the app does

The app helps donors find items that local organizations need and pledge to bring them. We don't handle money or donated items: each donation is between you and the organization you bring it to.

your account

  • You need to be at least 13 years old.
  • Use your own name or a name you're comfortable showing others, and keep your password safe.
  • You're responsible for what happens under your account.

if you're donating

  • Bring what you pledged during the drop-off window, or cancel the pledge so the organization isn't left waiting.
  • Follow the organization's rules about what it can accept, for example new or sealed items, and food safety rules.
  • Organizations can decline items that don't meet their needs.

if you represent an organization

  • Only register an organization you're authorized to represent, with accurate details.
  • Post needs that are real and current, and confirm drop-offs honestly.
  • We review organizations before they appear to donors, and may suspend any organization that doesn't follow these terms.

using the app fairly

Don't harass anyone, impersonate people or organizations, make fake pledges or needs, or try to break or misuse the app. We may suspend or remove accounts that do.

no guarantees

The app is provided "as is". We can't verify every need, pledge or drop-off, and we aren't responsible for donated items or for what happens between donors and organizations, to the extent the law allows.

ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app, under profile → delete account. See the privacy policy for what that removes.

changes

We may update these terms. If we make significant changes, we'll let you know in the app.
