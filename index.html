What it does

Built to close the gap between good intentions and real impact.

map
Live needs near you

The Give tab shows real, time-boxed needs on a map and in a list sorted by urgency, filtered by a travel distance you pick (1–50 miles). Expand the map to full screen and recenter on yourself anytime.

handshake
Pledges that add up

Donors pledge part of a need, and the database won't let pledges exceed what's still needed. Drop-off reminders keep pledges on track, and stale ones expire automatically every hour.

qr_code_2
QR code check-in

Each pledge has a check-in code. Staff scan it at drop-off (even with a phone's regular camera) to mark it received, and only received donations count toward badges and stats.

domain
Tools for organizations

Shelters register for admin approval, invite staff with codes, post needs from drafts, set needs to repeat on any schedule, and follow progress on a dashboard and public organization page.

emoji_events
Giving worth celebrating

Six milestone badges, a leaderboard you can opt out of, a community heat map, and a "year in giving" recap make impact visible without making it pushy.

share
Share & follow

Share any need with a link that opens a public page for friends without the app, and follow organizations to keep their needs at the top of your list.

How it works

From intention to action in four steps.

1
A need is posted

An approved organization posts something specific, like "40 pairs of new socks, drop off Tue 4–7pm."

2
Neighbors pledge

Donors nearby pledge part of it. Pledges can never exceed what's still needed.

3
Drop it off

The donor brings the items during the drop-off window and shows their check-in code.

4
Impact is counted

Staff mark the pledge received, and it counts toward the donor's badges, the leaderboard and community stats.

Live app

Try the real thing.

The preview above uses sample data. The real app runs in your browser with the same account and data as the phone version. Sample organizations are fictional, so feel free to explore.

Make an account

Sign up with an email and password, then enter the 6-digit code we email you. A quick walkthrough shows how the app works (reopen it anytime from the ? on Give).

Find a need

Set your travel distance on the Give tab and browse needs on the map or in the list.

Pledge & check in

Pledge what you can bring, then find your drop-off window and check-in code under My pledges.

See the bigger picture

Visit Community for the heat map and leaderboard, and Profile for badges and your year in giving.
