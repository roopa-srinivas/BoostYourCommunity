privacy policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

Boost Your Community is an app that helps people give what local shelters, food pantries and community fridges actually need. This policy explains what information the app collects, who can see it, and how to delete it. It's run by Roopa Srinivas; you can reach us by opening an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details such as your email address.

what we collect

  • Your account: your email address, the name you choose to show, and your password. Passwords are stored securely by our sign-in provider; we never see them.
  • Your pledges: what you pledged, to which need, when, and whether the organization confirmed it arrived.
  • Who you follow, so we can show your friends' leaderboard.
  • Organizations you register or work with: the organization's name, type, address and map location, and any description, phone number or website you add, plus who registered it and who is on its staff.

your location

To show needs near you, the app asks your device for your location and sends those coordinates along with the search. We use them to find nearby organizations and don't store your location. You can say no; the app will then show San Francisco instead.

who can see what

  • Your name is visible to other signed-in users, for example when they search for people to follow.
  • Your pledges are visible to you and to the staff of the organization you pledged to, including your name, so they know who to expect.
  • Your monthly total of confirmed items is visible to people who follow you, on their leaderboard.
  • The community heat map only shows totals per organization, never individual people.
  • App admins can see organizations that are waiting for approval, to check they're real.

services we use

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in, on servers in the United States.
  • Apple Maps or Google Maps show the map on your phone.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) finds the map location for an organization's address when it's registered in the web version of the app.

Drop-off reminders are scheduled on your own device; they aren't sent from our servers.

what we don't do

We don't show ads, we don't sell your information, and we don't use third-party tracking or analytics.

keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app, under profile → delete account. That permanently deletes your account, profile, pledges and follows. If you were the only staff member of an organization, the organization is suspended rather than deleted, so other donors' records stay intact; contact us if you'd like it removed.

children

The app isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app about any significant change.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub (issues are public, so please leave out personal details). · terms of use
